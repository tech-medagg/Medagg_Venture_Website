CASE STUDY

Healthcare Operational Turnaround & Strategy

Value Creation, Clinical MIS Cadence, and Margin Expansion for Multi-Specialty Hospital Ecosystems

Executive Overview

Medagg works towards redefining India's healthcare spectrum by designing structured operational solutions, managing clinical governance, arresting revenue leakage, and driving institutional valuation.

HIGHLIGHT 01

Clinical Workflow

Redesign of 40+ clinical and administrative processes to reduce turnaround time and elevate patient experience.

HIGHLIGHT 02

MIS Cadence

Weekly review rhythm and KPI accountability dashboards established across executive and clinical leadership.

HIGHLIGHT 03

Margin Expansion

Comprehensive supply chain management audit reducing cost of goods across pharmacy and diagnostic consumables.

Groundwork & As-Is Study

A comprehensive diagnosis of existing operations was executed across financial audits, staffing ratios, supply chain workflows, and accreditation compliance. Priority workstreams were isolated to arrest immediate revenue leaks.

Strategy & Implementation

Standard operating procedures were institutionalized with perpetual NABH readiness. Key star clinicians were onboarded to anchor high-margin specialty departments while marketing initiatives drove higher capacity utilization.

Outcomes & Measurable Impact

The engagement yielded a sustainable bottom-line turnaround, enhanced institutional reputation, robust governance cadence, and prepared the organization for next-stage strategic growth.
